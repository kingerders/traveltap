How It Works

Start Your Journey in Minutes

1

Download TravelTap

Get the app on your iPhone for free. No account required — just open and start exploring.

2

Explore Destinations

Browse attractions by continent, country, or city. Tap any place to see photos, descriptions, and location details.

3

Build Your Bucket List

Save the places you dream of visiting. Organize your travel goals and get inspired every day.

4

Track Your Adventures

Mark places as visited as you check them off. Watch your world map fill up with every new adventure.
