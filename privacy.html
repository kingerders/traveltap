Privacy Policy

How TravelTap handles your information

Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TravelTap ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how TravelTap collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our iOS application ("App").

By using TravelTap, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

TravelTap is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary to provide the service:

  • App Usage Data: Anonymous, aggregated data about how features are used (e.g., which sections are accessed). This data cannot be used to identify you.
  • In-App Purchase Data: When you make purchases within the App, payment processing is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.
  • Crash & Diagnostics Data: If you have opted into Apple's crash reporting, we may receive anonymized crash reports through Apple's developer tools to help improve App stability.
  • Saved Preferences: Your bucket list items, visited places, and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage mechanisms. This data does not leave your device unless you use iCloud backup.

We do not require account creation. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifiable information.

3. How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely to:

  • Improve App performance and stability
  • Understand general usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Process and restore in-app purchases via Apple's systems

4. Third-Party Services

TravelTap integrates the following Apple-provided frameworks that may have their own privacy practices:

  • Apple MapKit: Used for displaying maps and location information. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Apple StoreKit: Used for in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies.

We do not share your data with any other third-party companies, advertisers, or analytics services.

5. Data Retention

All personal data (such as your bucket list and preferences) is stored locally on your device. We do not store personal data on our servers. You may delete all App data at any time by uninstalling TravelTap from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

TravelTap is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect the limited information we do process. Your local device data is protected by your device's built-in security features (Face ID, passcode, etc.).

8. Your Rights

Since TravelTap does not collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal data we hold about you. For any questions or concerns about privacy, you may contact us at:

hello@traveltapapp.com

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
